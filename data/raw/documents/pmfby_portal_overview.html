Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY) — Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare

Scheme Overview

Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY) provides comprehensive crop insurance coverage against non-preventable natural risks from pre-sowing to post-harvest. Enrolment is voluntary for all farmers.

Farmer Premium Summary (Portal Snapshot)

Kharif Food Grains and Oilseeds: 2.0% of Sum Insured (or Actuarial rate, whichever is less). Rabi Food Grains and Oilseeds: 1.5% of Sum Insured (or Actuarial rate, whichever is less). Annual Commercial and Horticultural Crops: 5.0% of Sum Insured (or Actuarial rate, whichever is less).

Krishi Rakshak Portal Helpline: 14447. Localized loss intimation window: 72 hours.
