# FieldGuard Limitations and Known Boundaries

While FieldGuard achieves 94.05% classification accuracy, 94.11% macro-F1, and reduces false support to 2.63% (from 81.58% in standard numerical baselines), several methodological, engineering, and empirical limitations must be transparently acknowledged.

---

## 1. Domain and Corpus Scope
- **Scheme Concentration:** The primary evaluation corpus is concentrated on India's *Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana* (PMFBY). While PMFBY contains an exceptionally complex matrix of crop categories, seasonal rules, indemnity thresholds, and subsidy sharing ratios, policy dynamics in other welfare domains (such as direct cash transfers under PM-KISAN, or health insurance tariffs under Ayushman Bharat) feature different eligibility criteria, household means tests, and administrative hierarchies.
- **Corpus Size:** The active verified corpus contains 7 primary document manifests, 46 extracted evidence units, and 33 verified statutory rules. While this covers the complete core provisions of PMFBY operational guidelines, scaling to national repositories spanning thousands of federal and state notifications will require distributed index scaling.

---

## 2. Benchmark Construction & Synthetic Perturbation
- **Real vs. Synthetic Proportions:** The benchmark consists of 240 claims: 88 real-source claims and 152 systematically derived adversarial perturbations.
- **Purpose of Perturbations:** Adversarial perturbations are essential to evaluate safety-critical failure modes (wrong crop categories, inverted seasons, altered denominators, deleted qualifications) that do not frequently occur in genuine official documentation but are commonly generated by hallucinating LLMs in RAG pipelines.
- **Ecological Validity:** Although perturbations are derived from authentic source rules, real-world user queries may feature vaguer phrasing, dialectal variations, or multi-hop administrative dependencies not captured in clean synthetic templates.

---

## 3. Document and Table Extraction Complexities
- **Born-Digital vs. Scanned Documents:** The extraction pipeline relies on text and table stream parsing. Documents in the corpus are born-digital PDFs and HTML portals. For historical scanned gazette notifications requiring Optical Character Recognition (OCR), OCR character errors (e.g., confusing `2.0%` with `20%` or `1.5%` with `l.5%`) would propagate into evidence normalization.
- **Irregular and Nested Tables:** Multi-line cells with irregular column spans (e.g. footnotes spanning multiple columns) require manual table schema alignment in the evidence store. Fully autonomous table parsing across arbitrary PDF layouts remains an open challenge.

---

## 4. Conservative Abstention vs. Coverage Trade-Off
- **Conservative Bias:** FieldGuard is intentionally designed with an asymmetric safety loss: accepting a false entitlement claim in public insurance is far more hazardous than abstaining.
- **Coverage Impact:** On the held-out test set, FieldGuard exhibits an abstention rate of 17.86% (coverage: 82.14%). Most abstentions occur on queries asserting unreleased or unauthenticated future rules (e.g. 2026 post-revision claims). While this achieves a 0.0% false-support rate on unverifiable queries, users seeking best-effort heuristic guidance may perceive conservative abstention as non-responsiveness.

---

## 5. Temporal Supersession in Fragmented Policy Ecosystems
- When newer policy announcements (such as PIB press releases or pilot addenda) update a specific sub-clause without re-issuing the complete operational guidelines, establishing whether an unmentioned clause remains in force or has been superseded requires administrative gazette tracking. In the absence of an explicit supersession clause, FieldGuard returns `UNVERIFIABLE`.

---

## 6. Optional LLM Integration Limitations
- While FieldGuard's core pipeline is fully deterministic, offline-capable, and requires zero paid API tokens, optional LLM-assisted claim parsing (`Parser B`) is sensitive to API rate limits, provider outages, and non-deterministic JSON formatting errors. FieldGuard provides deterministic fallback (`Parser A`) to maintain offline reproducibility.
